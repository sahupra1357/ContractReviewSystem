PROPERTY PURCHASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES
This Purchase Agreement is made on 2026-02-12 between Acme Property Holdings LLC ("Seller") and Samuel Okafor ("Buyer").

2. PROPERTY
Seller agrees to sell the real property at 1200 Harbor View Drive, Seattle, WA, together with all fixtures and improvements (the "Property").

3. PURCHASE PRICE
The total purchase price is USD 702,000, payable at Closing by wire transfer to escrow account 4471-3920-0011.

4. EARNEST MONEY
Buyer shall deposit USD 17,000 as earnest money, which is non-refundable under all circumstances including failed contingencies.

5. CLOSING
Closing shall occur on or before 2026-11-08 at a title company selected by mutual agreement.

6. TITLE
Seller shall convey good and marketable title by general warranty deed, free of liens and encumbrances.

7. INSPECTION
Buyer may inspect the Property within 10 days of acceptance and may terminate if material defects are found and not remedied.

8. DEFAULT
If Buyer defaults, Seller's sole remedy is retention of the earnest money. If Seller defaults, Buyer may seek specific performance.

9. RISK OF LOSS
Risk of loss remains with Seller until Closing.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

11. SIGNATURES
Signed by Jordan Rivera for Seller and Samuel Okafor as Buyer on 2026-02-12.
